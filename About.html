ABOUT US



SKYGEAR


An iconic global sports and technology brand with an unwavering passion for racing - a passion that has resulted in more than five decades of success across the world's most competitive racing series. Our achievements include winning motorsport’s iconic Triple Crown.


In Formula 1 alone, we've won 20 world championships and 183 grand prix. And we've forged a formidable reputation off the track by building on our successful racing expertise to develop an automotive business that creates sportscars that are the epitome of luxury and high performance.


The official SkyGear Store is operated by Castore, an internationally recognized, licensed merchandise partner. This is Castore's entry into Formula 1 and motorsport, enabling us to deliver you the very latest officially licensed and technically advanced motorsport products.




ABOUT CASTORE


Castore's singular purpose is to elevate athletes to the next level. Our brand is founded on the philosophy of BETTER NEVER STOPS, a driving force that guides our every action and decision.


Our sportswear is crafted using advanced engineering and unique technical fabrics to create unparalleled quality. We cater to athletes who demand the best, and our innovative designs feature specialist features to optimize performance in all conditions.


We've partnered with SkyGear to design, develop, and distribute licensed motorsport merchandise worldwide. When you shop at SkyGear, you're dealing directly with the producers of the merchandise.
